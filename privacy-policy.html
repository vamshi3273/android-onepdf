1. Introduction

Welcome to OnePDF, an Android application designed to help you find, open, read and manage PDF documents on your device. The app may provide features such as recent documents, favorites, bookmarks, reading-position history, search within supported PDF documents, and display preferences.

This Privacy Policy describes what information the app may access, how it is used and stored, and when information may be processed by third-party services. By using OnePDF, you acknowledge this policy. If you have questions, contact us using the email address above.

2. Files and Permissions

OnePDF needs access to PDF documents that you choose to open or that the app is allowed to discover on your device. The exact access method depends on your Android version and the feature you use.

PDF documents and file access

When you select a PDF through Android's file picker, OnePDF receives access to the selected document. If you grant access to a folder, the app may use that access to find PDF files within the selected folder. Where Android permits persistent access, the app may retain the granted document access so that you can reopen the file later.

Where available and permitted, OnePDF may also use Android's MediaStore to list PDF documents indexed on your device. The app should request only the permissions needed for the file-discovery feature available in your installed version. Android may show a system permission prompt or document picker when access is needed.

Document content

PDF content is accessed to display pages and provide reader features. The app's core reading functionality is designed to work locally on your device. OnePDF does not need to upload the contents of your PDF documents to our own servers to provide basic PDF viewing.

Document names and details

To display your library and reading history, OnePDF may process document details such as file name, document URI or access reference, file size, last-modified date, page count when available, and your recent-reading or favorite status. These details are used to organize and reopen documents.

Search, bookmarks and reading progress

If supported and enabled, text search, bookmarks, the last-read page, and other reading preferences are processed to provide the corresponding reader features. These items are intended to be stored locally on your device.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed or created by OnePDF may be used to:

  • Find and display PDF documents you can access on your device.
  • Open and render PDF pages for reading.
  • Show recent documents and favorites.
  • Save bookmarks and reading position where those features are available.
  • Remember app preferences, such as theme, display mode and sorting choices.
  • Provide document search and other features included in the installed version.
  • Display and measure advertisements through third-party advertising services.
  • Respond to support requests that you send to us and maintain app security and reliability.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

OnePDF is designed as an offline-first PDF reader. PDF documents that you open remain in their original location on your device or in the storage provider you selected. The app may store document references, reading history, favorites, bookmarks and preferences locally so that its features work correctly.

We do not operate a server that is required to upload or store your PDF documents for the app's core reading features. We do not sell your personal information. We do not send the contents of your PDFs or your locally stored reading history to our own servers unless a future feature clearly explains this and you choose to use it.

If you choose to share a PDF using Android's share functionality, the document is passed to the app or destination you select. That recipient's privacy practices will apply to information you share with them.

Local app data may remain on your device until you remove it, clear OnePDF's app data, or uninstall the app. Android backup or device-transfer behavior may be governed by your device settings and Android services.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

OnePDF may display advertisements using Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location signals, app interactions, diagnostics and information needed to deliver, measure, limit or personalize advertisements. The information processed depends on your region, device settings, consent choices, the Google Mobile Ads SDK version and the app's configuration.

OnePDF does not control the data practices of third-party providers. Please review Google's policies for more information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google AdMob and user data

The app may also use Google Play services for app distribution or other platform functionality. Those services process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Internet Connection

Opening and reading PDF files stored on your device is intended to work without an internet connection. Internet access may be used for advertising, app updates, or other network-dependent services that are included in the installed version. The presence of internet access does not mean that your PDF documents are uploaded.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by OnePDF. Locally stored information is subject to your device's security and Android's protections. Information processed by third-party SDKs is subject to the security measures and practices of those providers. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Your Choices and Data Deletion

  • File access: You can manage app permissions in Android settings. You can also revoke access to selected files or folders through Android's system controls where available. If access is revoked, OnePDF may no longer be able to open the affected document until you select it again.
  • Local app data: You can remove items using available app controls, clear OnePDF's app data, or uninstall the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove locally stored favorites, bookmarks, reading history and preferences.
  • Advertising: You can manage available advertising, consent and privacy settings through your device, Google account, or consent choices presented in the app, where applicable.
  • Support: Contact us at the email address at the top of this policy with privacy questions or requests concerning information that we control.

9. Children's Privacy

OnePDF is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support channel, please contact us so we can review the request.
